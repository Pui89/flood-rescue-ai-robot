How we work

Open core. Evidence before claims.

The goal is a sustainable robotics engineering business that keeps a useful open-source core while offering paid support and integration when the work is ready.

  • Open-source core and transparent project documentation
  • Optional paid integration, deployment and engineering support
  • Reproducible benchmark protocols and versioned releases
  • Human supervision and explicit safety boundaries
  • Licensing and model-weight provenance reviewed before release
Commercial model

Research → pilot → supported deployment

Potential offerings can include research collaboration, system integration, Jetson edge deployment, custom evaluation and ongoing technical support. Scope and pricing should be agreed per project after requirements and readiness are assessed.

No unsupported promises. We do not claim certification, guaranteed rescue outcomes or proven field performance without independent evidence.

Engineering roadmap

Earn each stage with evidence.

Milestones are gates, not marketing dates. Release status should advance only when the required tests and artifacts exist.

01 / FOUNDATION

Define & reproduce

Lock scope, licenses, hardware/software versions and baseline replay.

02 / MVP

Map & evaluate

Build sensor-health checks, hazard evidence and benchmark outputs.

03 / EDGE PILOT

Validate on device

Measure sustained latency, memory, power, temperature and failures.

04 / CONTROLLED USE

Review & support

Complete risk review, operator procedures, HIL tests and release gates.
