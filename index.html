PUI89 Flood Recon is a research-to-product robotics platform for supervised flood reconnaissance, hazard mapping, and field evidence collection—designed for edge compute on NVIDIA Jetson Orin-class systems.

01 / Platform

Reconnaissance first. Safety by design.

The initial product is a supervised sensing and mapping system—not an autonomous rescue promise. It turns sensor observations into traceable hazard hypotheses and maps that an operator can review.

CAPABILITY 01

Multimodal perception

Fuse camera, depth, thermal, LiDAR, IMU and GNSS inputs when supported by the selected hardware. Track sensor health and report missing or stale data.

CAPABILITY 02

Hazard-aware mapping

Build a spatial view of observed hazards, traversability evidence, confidence and unknown regions. Preserve timestamps and source evidence for review.

CAPABILITY 03

Edge deployment

Target NVIDIA Jetson Orin / AGX Orin with pinned software versions, offline-capable operation, resource monitoring and reproducible benchmarks.

02 / System design

Perception can be uncertain. Safety logic cannot.

The architecture separates probabilistic AI from deterministic health checks and motion permissions. Unknown or stale information should reduce autonomy, not silently become a safe-path assumption.

01
Sensor contracts & health gateTimestamp, calibration, freshness, confidence and availability.
02
Perception & trackingDetect, segment and track objects; flag out-of-distribution inputs.

Deployment principle

Start with recorded-data replay and offline mapping. Move to bench and hardware-in-the-loop testing before controlled field trials. Enable robot motion only after safety gates and emergency-stop behavior have been independently tested.

04 / Delivery

Stage gates from simulation to field use.

Progress is measured by evidence and exit criteria, not calendar promises. A stage does not pass when required tests are missing.

STAGE 1 · BASELINE

Reproducible core

Pin dependencies, define interfaces, document licenses and make replay tests deterministic.

STAGE 2 · MAPPING

Offline recon MVP

Produce timestamped maps and hazard layers with confidence, provenance and unknown areas.

STAGE 3 · EDGE PILOT

Jetson validation

Record sustained latency, FPS, memory, power, temperature and recovery behavior.

STAGE 4 · FIELD GATE

Controlled trials

Complete risk review, operator training, emergency-stop checks and fault-injection evidence.

05 / Safety boundary

Support responders—not replace their judgement.

Prototype safety noticeThis project is not certified for life-critical use. Do not deploy it in floodwater, near people, or in public operations without a documented risk assessment, trained human supervision, validated emergency-stop systems, and applicable approvals. A hazard map is decision support, not a guarantee of safety.

AI outputs may be wrong or incomplete. Water glare, turbidity, occlusion, communication loss and changing terrain can invalidate a map. Unknown areas must remain marked unknown.
